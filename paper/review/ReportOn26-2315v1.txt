Three reviewers reviewed the paper, and the overall evaluation is
negative. The major concerns are: (1) the claim that the LLM can
replace the GNN object scorer is overstated; (2) the generalization
claim is not well demonstrated as all experiments are on a single
domain (MazeNamo), without cross-domain evidence; (3) the scalability
claim is not fully supported; (4) the three stages are not equally
supported: stage 1 is convincing, stage 2 is explicitly neutral, and
stage 3 has mixed results; (5) the comparison between manual rules and
LLM-generated rules is not fair; (6) the stage 3 failure case needs
more analysis; and (7) some figures and statements are not consistent.
